# Systematic review and meta-analysis of regulator-approved deep learning systems for fundus diabetic retinopathy detections

**Citation:** Wang TW, Luo WT, Tu YK, Chou YB, Wu YT. Systematic review and meta-analysis of regulator-approved deep learning systems for fundus diabetic retinopathy detections. npj Digital Medicine. 2025;9(1):110. doi: 10.1038/s41746-025-02223-8. Venue: npj Digital Medicine. **Peer reviewed.**
**Year:** 2025
**Dataset(s) used:** 82 evaluation datasets covering 887,244 examinations, 25 devices and 28 countries, from studies conducted between 2016 and 2025
**Model(s)/method(s):** Hierarchical bivariate meta-analysis with meta-regression. Regulator-approved deep learning diabetic retinopathy screening systems.

## Summary (plain English)
This is the big-picture summary of the whole field. A meta-analysis pools results from many separate studies to get one overall estimate. The authors restricted themselves to systems that regulators have actually approved, so this reflects products in real use rather than research prototypes. They also ran meta-regression, which tests which study features explain differences in results. Roughly two-thirds of the studies came from high-income countries and one-third from middle-income settings such as India, China, Thailand and Mexico.

## Key finding (plain English)
Pooled performance was strong, with 0.93 sensitivity and 0.90 specificity per patient, close to expert grading. But only 36 of 82 studies, 44%, used a fully external test set from a different country or ethnic population, and results varied with disease severity threshold, national income level, image gradability and pupil dilation.
